Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic account information such as your name and email address. When you use the Service, we collect the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic usage information, such as login activity, to help keep your account secure.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service — that means displaying your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and keeping your account secure. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are only accessible through your authenticated account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers, such as hosting or font services, to operate the Service. These providers process data only as necessary to deliver their service to us and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
